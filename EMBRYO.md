# Embryo Power Guard

Embryo Power Guard is Embryo-Zero's fork of [nut](https://github.com/networkupstools/nut) (1c4ab78, commit 1c4ab78). It does UPS battery monitoring and safe shutdown.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING, LICENSE-DCO, LICENSE-GPL2, LICENSE-GPL3) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
